What is Present Continuous with Future?

It is an expression used to talk about actions that will happen in the near future, or actions that will be in progress in the future.

The Rule:

will be + V-ing

Example: I will be studying at 8 PM tonight.

The goal of this activity is to understand how to use this rule in English sentences.
